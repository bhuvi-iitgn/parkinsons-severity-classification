Parkinson's Disease Severity Classification

Predicting UPDRS clinical severity tiers (Mild, Moderate, Severe) on unseen patients from sustained-vowel voice biomarkers.

Open Live ML App (Streamlit) GitHub Source Code

1. Question & Problem Framing

  • Question: Can remote voice recordings classify Parkinson's severity on unseen speakers?
  • Row & Target: 1 row = 1 sustained-vowel recording. Target total_UPDRS is grouped into Mild (≤24), Moderate (24–34), and Severe (>34).
  • Inputs: age, sex, and acoustic biomarkers only (motor_UPDRS and subject# excluded to prevent leakage).

2. Data & Preprocessing

  • Dataset: UCI Parkinson's Telemonitoring (5,875 recordings, 42 patients, 0 missing values; early-stage cohort without healthy controls).
  • Feature Engineering & Scaling: Created 31 features including clinical interactions (age_dfa, age_ppe, ppe_to_hnr). StandardScaler is fit strictly inside training folds.

3. Baseline, Methods & Evaluation

  • Validation Split: StratifiedGroupKFold by subject# tests generalization across 42 unseen patients with zero speaker overlap.
  • Model & Metrics: Compared untuned Random Forest baseline against a regularized, class-balanced Random Forest (class_weight="balanced", max_depth=6). Evaluated via Macro F1 and Severe-Stage F1 rather than raw accuracy alone.

6. Errors, Limitations & Conclusion

  • Errors & Limitations: Misclassifications cluster near the 24 and 34 UPDRS cutoffs. Vocal features correlate with severity but do not prove causation or replace clinical diagnosis.
  • Conclusion: Grouping by patient and pairing age-biomarker interactions with balanced regularization solves Severe-class collapse.
