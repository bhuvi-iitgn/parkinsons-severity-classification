Parkinson's Disease Severity Classification

Non-invasive UPDRS severity triage (Mild, Moderate, Severe) from sustained-vowel voice biomarkers using patient-grouped Random Forest modeling.

Launch Live ML Dashboard (Streamlit) View Source Repository on GitHub

1. Research Question & Problem Framing

Question: Can sustained-vowel vocal biomarkers accurately triage clinical Parkinson's disease severity on unseen patients without requiring in-person clinical scoring?

  • Observation Unit: One sustained-vowel phonation recording (5,875 recordings across 42 patients).
  • Target Variable: Clinical total_UPDRS discretized into Mild (≤ 24), Moderate (24–34), and Severe (> 34).
  • Prediction-Time Inputs: Patient age, biological sex, and acoustic biomarkers only. motor_UPDRS and subject# are excluded to prevent target and speaker-identity leakage.

Source & Limits: UCI Machine Learning Repository Parkinson's Telemonitoring Dataset (Tsanas et al., 2009). Measures pitch perturbation (Jitter, PPE), amplitude perturbation (Shimmer), turbulent airflow (DFA, RPDE), and harmonic-to-noise clarity (HNR, NHR). Limited to 42 early-stage Parkinson's subjects (no healthy controls).

3. Leakage-Free Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

  • Biomedical Interactions (31 Features): Engineered domain interaction terms including age_dfa, age_ppe, and ppe_to_hnr to capture age-modulated vocal instability.
  • Strict Fold Isolation: StandardScaler and feature transformations are fit strictly on training folds inside cross-validation before transforming unseen validation patients.

4. Baselines, Methods & Evaluation Design

  • Validation Scheme: StratifiedGroupKFold grouped by subject# ensures all recordings from a given patient appear exclusively in either train or validation folds (testing generalization across all 42 unseen speakers).
  • Metric Choice: Untuned models achieve ~40.8% raw accuracy simply by over-predicting the majority Moderate tier while collapsing on Severe patients (0.0400 F1). Macro F1 and Severe-Stage F1 serve as primary metrics.
  • Final Model: Regularized, class-balanced Random Forest (class_weight="balanced", max_depth=6, min_samples_leaf=10).

7. Error Analysis, Limitations & Conclusion

  • Representative Errors: Misclassifications concentrate near the continuous UPDRS cutoffs (24 and 34), where adjacent scores share overlapping acoustic signatures, alongside a precision–recall trade-off on borderline Moderate cases due to class balancing.
  • Limitations & Non-Causation: Vocal biomarkers correlate with disease severity and aid remote triage, but do not establish biological causation or substitute for clinical diagnosis.
  • Conclusion: Patient-grouped validation combined with age-biomarker interactions and class-balanced regularization enables reliable multi-tier Parkinson's severity screening on unseen speakers.
